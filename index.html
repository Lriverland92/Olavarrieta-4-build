Bilbao

Bilbao is a city in northern Spain, located in the Basque Country. It is known for its combination of traditional Basque culture and modern architecture. One of its most famous attractions is the Guggenheim Museum, which helped transform the city into an important destination for art and tourism. Bilbao is also known for its historic Old Town, local cuisine, and its location along the Nervión River.

Toledo

Toledo is a historic city in central Spain, located in the region of Castilla-La Mancha. It is known for its medieval architecture and its history as a place where Christian, Jewish, and Muslim cultures lived together. Some of its most famous attractions include the Toledo Cathedral and the Alcázar of Toledo. The city is also closely connected to the artist El Greco, who lived and worked there for many years.

Toledo's official website

Sevilla

Sevilla is a city in southern Spain and the capital of Andalusia. It is known for its architecture, flamenco traditions, and rich history. Some of its most famous attractions include the Sevilla Cathedral, the Real Alcázar, and Plaza de España. The city is also known for its festivals, traditional neighborhoods, and strong influence on Spanish culture.
